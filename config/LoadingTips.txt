## MODPACK
Type 'discord' in terminal to suggest modpack changes.
Found a bug? Type 'discord' in terminal to report it.
Enjoying it? Type 'modpack' and hit that like button!
Soft-lock? Press CTRL+ALT+SHIFT+Q to return to main menu.
## TERMINAL
The terminal home page lists all available commands.
Use arrow keys to switch between player cameras in terminal.
Type 'bestiary' to read about enemies and their behavior.
Type 'refund' to cancel a dropship order before it lands.
## GAMEPLAY
Large bodies of water can be fatal.
Fire exits blocked by planks can be broken with any weapon.
Crouching reduces the noise you make while moving.
Dropping heavy items makes a loud noise that can attract enemies.
Lobby re-opens automatically when returning to orbit.
Flashlights flicker and dim below 30% battery.
Gifts can sometimes be lethal.
Level 2 visor crack increases oxygen consumption.
## Dig through snow on snowy moons to clear paths.
On Flooded weather, water slowly rises inside the facility.
Leaving the moon early requires 60% of players to vote.
Use spray paint to mark paths inside large facilities.
Press [ M ] to mute yourself.
Press [ P ] while spectating to toggle first person view.
Scroll wheel zooms the third person camera while spectating.
The company buying rate is highest on the last day of a quota.
Items left on the moon are permanently lost when you leave orbit.
## ENEMIES
Isolated players are much easier targets for most enemies.
Staring too long at Bracken makes him angry.
## Bracken drags grabbed players toward its favorite spot.
Eyeless dogs are completely blind, don't make any noise close to them.
Thumper can be outrun by sprinting and cutting around corners.
Forest Giant can be distracted by a playing boombox.
Snare Flea on your head can only be knocked off by a crewmate.
Shoot Nutcrackers eye when it opens to deal damage.
Jester can't be stopped once it pops, leave the facility immediately.
Coil-Head stops moving when looked at directly.
Playing boombox speeds up Hygroderes but stops their damage.
Stand on railings to stay safe from Hygroderes.
Zap-gun can kill Hygroderes.
Damaging a Hygrodere slightly increases its speed.
Sprint jumping over a Hygrodere is possible with enough space.
Circuit bees attack enemies that get too close to the hive.
Circuit bees can set Forest Keepers on fire.
Earth Leviathans hunt large enemies but ignore smaller prey.
Masked enemy mask can be picked up and sold after killing them.
Some enemies can trigger landmines and be targeted by turrets.
Barber attacks in rhythm.
## ITEMS
Shovel, stop sign and yield sign all deal the same melee damage.
Stun grenade temporarily stuns most enemies.
Zap-gun requires two people: one to aim and one to hold the enemy still.
Jetpack speed and handling drop significantly under heavy weight.
Radar booster can be pinged from the terminal to locate it.
Zap-gun temporarily disables landmines.
Apparatus removal cuts all power and radiation damages all crew over time.
Locked doors break with melee weapons, shotguns or landmines.
Security doors open via terminal, Zap-gun,or during a power outage.
## CRUISER
Items on top of the Cruiser or in the open trunk are lost when entering orbit.
The Cruiser takes damage from hitting trees and walls.
## MOONS
Landing on a free moon twice permanently discovers it.
Landing on a paid moon once permanently discovers it.
Paid moons have a chance to go on sale after each quota.
Embrion and Artifice must be story-unlocked before routing to them.
Systems unlock through moon landings, quota survival or specific moon visits
## QUOTA
Each unrecovered dead player reduces credits by 5%.
2 or more unrecovered deaths increase next quota by 3%.
Quota scales up for each additional player beyond 2.
